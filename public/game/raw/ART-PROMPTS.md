# Image brief — read this first, then wait

You are generating 12 images for a 2D game. This file contains all 12 prompts.

**How to work through them:**

1. Generate **one image per reply**, in the order listed. Never more than one.
2. After each image, stop and wait for me to say "next". Do not continue on your
   own. I may ask you to redo one before moving on.
3. Use the prompt **exactly as written**. Do not shorten it, do not rewrite it,
   do not merge it with the previous one, and do not "improve" the wording. Each
   prompt repeats the same style paragraph on purpose — that repetition is what
   keeps 12 separately generated images looking like one world. It is not an
   error and must not be collapsed.
4. Each prompt says **square**, **landscape** or **portrait**. Use that shape.
5. Where a prompt asks for a **flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background**, that
   is a literal instruction. Paint actual magenta pixels. Do **not** substitute a
   transparent background, and do **not** draw a checkerboard or any other
   "transparency" pattern — a script removes the magenta afterwards, and a
   checkerboard breaks it. If you believe transparency would be better, you are
   wrong for this job; paint the magenta.
6. **Nothing in the artwork may be magenta, pink or hot purple.** That colour is
   reserved for the background and is cut out of the final image. Anything
   painted in it will be destroyed.
7. For images 8 and 9, I will upload image 7 first. Match that creature exactly —
   same body, same markings, same proportions, same colours — and change only the
   pose.

Say "ready" and nothing else. Then I'll say "1" and you generate the first image.
